Decomposition of the 3-Way Scenario Curves & Hydrological Components

A precise scientific description of each component presented in the trajectory graph and scenario triad above, outlining their biophysical origin, mathematical definition, and operational significance.

📍

1. Origin Datum (0, 0) at Year 2025 (X-Axis Intercept)

The Cartesian coordinate origin $(0, 0)$ is pinned directly at calendar year 2025 on the horizontal X-axis. This represents the empirical calibration boundary where 26 consecutive years of authentic Earth observation satellite records (2000–2025, totaling 300,232 authentic observations) terminate. All three forecast curves physically originate and diverge from this datum point, guaranteeing that future projections are firmly anchored to observed climatology rather than ungrounded assumptions.
📏

2. Crop Water Footprint (CWF) Metric (Y-Axis)

The vertical Y-axis evaluates the consumptive Crop Water Footprint ($m^3/\text{ton}$) defined by the Hoekstra Water Footprint Network protocol: $CWF = CWU / Y$, where $CWU$ is cumulative crop water use ($m^3/\text{ha}$) and $Y$ is harvested crop yield ($\text{ton}/\text{ha}$). Depending on the selected reporting basis, it spans normalized unit scale (4–7 m³/ton), commercial refined sugar (1,490–2,410 m³/ton), or fresh cane biomass (112–314 m³/ton).
📈

3. The 3 Quantile Scenario Curves Extending from 2025

Rather than producing an oversimplified single average, three physical quantile trajectories extend outward across the chosen forecast horizon:

  • 🟡 Drought Scenario Curve (Upper Divergence, 18% Probability): Total CWF surges under severe moisture deficit, forcing intense transpiration stress while soil moisture rapidly depletes to wilting point.
  • 🟢 Normal / Baseline Curve (Central Equilibrium, 64% Probability): Total CWF follows the 50th climatological percentile with balanced crop evapotranspiration ($ET_c$) and optimal yield development.
  • 🔵 Flood Scenario Curve (Lower Blue Bound, 18% Probability): Heavy monsoonal precipitation supersaturates the root zone, reducing blue irrigation demand to zero while risking root zone anoxia.
🎨

4. Dual-Color Curve Partitioning (Blue vs. Green Length)

Each curve is physically rendered with two contiguous color segments whose respective arc lengths strictly equal their volumetric water contributions ($L_{total} = L_{blue} + L_{green}$):

  • 🔵 Blue Water ($CWF_{blue}$): Rendered in electric blue. Measures the artificial volume of freshwater withdrawn from surface rivers, irrigation canals, and deep groundwater aquifers.
  • 🟢 Green Water ($CWF_{green}$): Rendered in emerald green. Measures natural precipitation stored within the soil root zone and transpired by crop canopies.
⚠️

5. Multi-Hazard Agronomic Directives & Economic Loss

Operational decisions derived directly below each trajectory curve:

  • 🚨 Irrigation Directives: Triggers immediate high-frequency drip scheduling or drainage control.
  • 📉 Stewart Harvest Loss: Non-linear yield collapse calculated via FAO-33 water-yield functions ($K_y$).
  • 💸 Financial Revenue Loss: Quantifies farmer revenue deficit benchmarked against statutory prices.
  • 💧 Capillary Ground Support: Accounts for natural upward hydraulic flux from the shallow alluvial water table.
